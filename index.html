01 / WHO WE ARE

Freedom grows through cooperation, not command.

TAC is a decentralised, voluntary association of independent digital communities, physical collectives, labour unions and individuals committed to libertarian socialism, anti-authoritarianism and mutual aid.

The confederation exists to enable connection and collective action while respecting the autonomy of its participating nodes and members.

08 / HOW WE ORGANISE

Delegation is a task,
not a throne.

The General Assembly is the federation-wide deliberative forum for active membership. Autonomous nodes retain their own self-organisation. The Supervision Council performs the procedural and liaison duties defined by the charter; it is not a central command over nodes.

See transparency principles →
General Assembly Active membership · confederation-wide deliberation
mandates · coordination · accountability
Autonomous nodes Local self-organisation
Supervision Council Defined procedural role · no command authority

A conceptual relationship, not a hierarchy of command.
